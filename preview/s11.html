ALTA & Men's Health

Alta Male Lab was never meant to be only a journal.

Movember

Every November since 2020, I take part in Movember.

Not as a brand activation. But as a personal commitment to a cause that matters.

Prostate cancer. Testicular cancer. Mental health and suicide prevention.

Issues that deserve greater awareness, research, and support.

Through the Alta Male Lab Movember Team, I invite others to contribute to that effort.
