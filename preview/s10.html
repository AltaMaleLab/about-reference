A Journal for the Second Half of Life

Most health content is built for youth, performance, or rapid transformation.

Alta Male Lab focuses on something else:
the second half of a man's life.

The years where strength must be maintained, not assumed.

Where energy must be managed, not taken for granted.

Where clarity becomes more valuable than intensity.

The Long Game is learning to navigate these years with intelligence.
